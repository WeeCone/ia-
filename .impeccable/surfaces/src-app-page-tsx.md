# Surface brief: public site (homepage, pricing, auth, legal)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: French retail traders deciding whether a paid AI chart reading is worth 40 €/mois. Action: drop a chart / subscribe; price stated beside every action (paid only, no trial). Proof: the product's own output (annotated chart, verdict, plan), the blurred BTC demo video (kept). No testimonials until real ones exist. Constraint: no gain promises; "pas un conseil en investissement" visible near the verdict.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an annotated game: Scanzivo comments the trader's chart the way a chess annotator comments a position, ending on one evaluation symbol. It refuses the dark neon AI-SaaS page (glow, gradient words, icon bento, split hero).

OWN-WORLD: Club roll-up board green field (committed, 30-60% of the page), paper-white sheets, true-black ink, annotation red for "?" and bearish marks. Informator-style evaluation symbols (± ∓ = ∞) as the verdict system with a printed legend. High-contrast display serif for titles, a sturdy text face, mono for prices and move numbers. Hairline diagram frames with coordinates; no glow, no gradients, no icon tiles.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of chart turned into a diagram with a numbered line of analysis and one symbol; understands the four verdicts; believes it commits and stays honest ("=" attendre exists); knows it costs 40 €/mois; drops a chart or picks an offer.
